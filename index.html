About

A CSM who thinks like a product lead

I came to customer success from corporate travel, where I ran client accounts and coached account managers into their first leadership roles. Since then I've worked with enterprise customers at Gainsight, Miro and Zendesk, leading QBRs with executive sponsors and turning what customers tell me into product feedback that lands.

Along the way I started building. I helped customers design and launch AI agents at Zendesk, and on my own time I've shipped an AI assistant for CSMs, a browser extension, and a book. I like taking an idea from a rough sketch to something people can use.

I'm open to customer success roles and to work on innovation teams, where understanding customers and building for them are the same job.
